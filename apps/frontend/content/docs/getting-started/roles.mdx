VidyaSchool uses role-based access control (RBAC) to ensure each user only sees and interacts with tools relevant to their responsibilities.

## Important Warnings

> [!WARNING]
> Never share your login credentials. Each user is responsible for activity under their account.

> [!WARNING]
> Students attempting to access teacher or admin routes will be redirected to the Unauthorized page.

## Student Role

Students have read-only access to their own academic data. Capabilities include:
• View personal marks, report cards, and GPA.
• View and pay fee ledger installments.
• Access the library catalog and track borrowed books.
• Read class notices and school circulars.
• File complaint/support tickets directed at teachers or administration.
• Update personal profile details (address, contact info).
Students cannot view other students' data, submit grades, or publish notices.

## Teacher Role

Teachers have read-write access to classroom tools. Capabilities include:
• View and manage the class roster for assigned classes.
• Enter, edit, and submit student marks for all exam types.
• Publish notices and announcements to specific classes.
• Submit leave requests and supply orders to administration.
• Receive and respond to student complaint tickets.
• View student profiles (contact info, commute details) for their classes only.

## Accounts Clerk Role

Accounts clerks manage financial operations. Capabilities include:
• Create, edit, and delete fee ledger entries for all students.
• Record and verify manual payment transactions.
• Apply scholarship concessions and waivers.
• Generate and download batch fee reports.
• View student financial history and outstanding balances.

## Administrator Role

Administrators have full system access. Capabilities include:
• Approve or reject teacher registration requests.
• Manage all user accounts (create, suspend, delete).
• Access all data across all roles.
• Manage class and section configurations.
• View complaint tickets from all sources and assign resolutions.
• Access system-wide reports and analytics.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can a teacher also be an accounts clerk?
No — roles are mutually exclusive. A single account can only hold one role. If a staff member has dual responsibilities, they would need two separate accounts (one per role).

### Who do I contact if my role permissions seem wrong?
Contact your school administrator. They can audit your role assignment and correct any discrepancies in the admin panel.
