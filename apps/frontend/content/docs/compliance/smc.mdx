# School Management Committee (SMC)

The School Management Committee (SMC) of The VIDYA School is constituted in strict accordance with the Affiliation Bye-Laws of the Central Board of Secondary Education (CBSE) and the Right to Education (RTE) Act. The committee ensures transparent governance, academic vigor, student safety, and holistic welfare.
