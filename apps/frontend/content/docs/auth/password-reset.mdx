How to securely reset a forgotten password and recover access to your VidyaSchool account.

## Steps

### Step 01: Click 'Forgot Password'
On the login page, click the 'Forgot Password?' link below the password field. This opens the password reset form.

### Step 02: Enter your Registered Email
Enter the email address you used to register your account. The system validates that an account with that email exists before sending the reset link.

### Step 03: Check your Inbox
A password reset email is sent within 1–2 minutes. The email contains a time-limited reset link (valid for 1 hour). Click the link to open the password reset form.

### Step 04: Set a New Password
Enter and confirm your new password. It must be at least 8 characters. Avoid reusing your previous password. Click 'Reset Password' to save. You'll be automatically redirected to the login page.

## Important Warnings

> [!WARNING]
> Password reset links expire after 1 hour. Request a new one if yours has expired.

> [!WARNING]
> If you registered with Google or GitHub (social login), you don't have a VidyaSchool password. Log in using your social provider.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### I didn't receive the reset email. What should I do?
Check your spam folder. If it's not there after 5 minutes, request another reset link. Make sure you're entering the email you registered with.

### I use social login and can't reset my password here. What do I do?
Social login accounts don't use VidyaSchool passwords. To reset your access, use the password reset option on your Google or GitHub account settings page.
