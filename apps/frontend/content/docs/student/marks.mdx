How to access your test results, understand the weighted grading system, navigate term-wise performance sheets, and read teacher feedback.

## Tips

- Check marks regularly after each assessment — teachers publish results within 3–5 working days of the exam.
- If you believe a mark is incorrect, raise a re-valuation request through your class teacher within 7 days of publication.

## Navigating the Marks Portal

Access Marks from your student sidebar. The marks portal displays all published assessment results organised by:
• Academic Year (e.g., 2024–25)
• Term (Term 1 / Mid-Term / Term 2 / Annual)
• Subject (Mathematics, Physics, English, etc.)
Use the filter dropdowns to narrow by term or subject. Results appear only after your teacher has published them — unpublished assessments show a 'Results Pending' placeholder.

## Understanding the Weighted Grading System

VidyaSchool uses a weighted grading model for final subject percentages:
• Assignments/Class Tests: 20% weight
• Mid-Term Examinations: 30% weight
• Term Final Examinations: 50% weight

Example: If you scored 80/100 in Assignments, 70/100 in Mid-Term, and 85/100 in Finals for Mathematics:
Weighted Score = (80 × 0.20) + (70 × 0.30) + (85 × 0.50) = 16 + 21 + 42.5 = 79.5%

The cumulative GPA is computed across all subjects at the end of each term using the standard 10-point GPA scale.

## Reading the Report Card

The full-term report card is accessible from the 'Report Card' tab. It shows:
• Subject-wise marks in each assessment category
• Weighted final percentage per subject
• Grade letter (A+, A, B+, B, C, D, F)
• Cumulative GPA for the term
• Class rank and percentile
• Teacher remarks per subject
• Overall conduct and attendance notes
Report cards require parent sign-off, which is tracked digitally. The portal will alert you when your parent needs to acknowledge the card.

## Grade Scale Reference

The letter grade scale used by VidyaSchool:
• 90–100%: A+ (Outstanding)
• 80–89%: A (Excellent)
• 70–79%: B+ (Very Good)
• 60–69%: B (Good)
• 50–59%: C (Satisfactory)
• 40–49%: D (Needs Improvement)
• Below 40%: F (Fail — Re-examination Required)
Subjects with an F grade require a supplementary examination. Contact your class teacher for re-exam scheduling.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### My marks are published for some subjects but not others. Is this normal?
Yes — each subject teacher publishes marks independently. Results appear as they are submitted. If a subject's results are more than 2 weeks late, inform your class teacher.

### How is my GPA calculated if I'm missing one assessment?
Missing assessments are recorded as 0/max marks, which heavily impacts the weighted score. If you were absent for a valid reason (medical, etc.), speak to your teacher about a make-up assessment.
