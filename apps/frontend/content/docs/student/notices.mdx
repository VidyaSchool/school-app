How to access class notices, school-wide circulars, and urgent announcements published by teachers and administration.

## Tips

- Enable browser notifications to receive alerts for new notices without checking the portal manually.
- Check urgent notices immediately — they often contain time-sensitive information like exam venue changes.

## Types of Notices

The Notices section aggregates all announcements relevant to you:
• Class Notices: Published by your class teacher or subject teachers — homework, exam schedules, assignment deadlines.
• School Circulars: Published by administration — fee reminders, holiday announcements, event notifications.
• Urgent Alerts: Flagged as urgent by the publisher and displayed with a high-visibility warning badge at the top of your notice board.
Notices are sorted newest-first. Unread notices are highlighted until opened.

## Filtering & Searching Notices

Use the filter panel to narrow notices by:
• Source: All, Class Notices, School Circulars
• Date range: This week, This month, Custom range
• Subject: Filter by subject-specific notices
Use the search bar to find specific notices by keyword in the title or content.

## Attachments & Downloads

Notices may include downloadable attachments such as PDFs (question papers, syllabus documents, event forms) or images (timetables, event posters). Click the attachment icon to download directly to your device.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### I'm not seeing notices from a particular subject. Why?
Notices are class-specific. Ensure your class and section were set correctly during onboarding. If they were, the teacher may not have published any notices yet for that subject.
