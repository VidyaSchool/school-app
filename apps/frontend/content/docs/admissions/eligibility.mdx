# Admission Eligibility Criteria & Age Guidelines

Criteria and minimum age requirements as prescribed by the Directorate of Education and CBSE for entry into various classes.

| Grade Level | Minimum Age (as of March 31) | Academic Prerequisites | Class Section Size |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Nursery / Balvatika | 3+ Years | Foundational physical & communicative readiness | 25 - 30 Students |
| Kindergarten (KG) | 4+ Years | Basic pre-literacy and pre-numeracy familiarity | 30 Students |
| Class I | 6+ Years | Foundational literacy & basic cognitive readiness | 35 Students |
| Classes II –  VIII | Age-appropriate (6+ to 14+ Years) | Passing marks in previous grade from recognized school | 35 - 40 Students |
| Classes IX – XI | 15+ Years | CBSE / State Board passing report card and TC | 35 - 40 Students |
