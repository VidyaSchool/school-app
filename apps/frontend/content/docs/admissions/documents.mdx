Complete checklist of documents required for a successful admissions application to VidyaSchool.

## Important Warnings

> [!WARNING]
> Submission of fraudulent or forged documents will result in immediate cancellation of admission and may lead to legal consequences.

> [!WARNING]
> Ensure the Transfer Certificate is from the student's most recent school — TCs from schools attended more than one academic year ago require an explanation letter.

## For New Class 1 Admissions

The following original documents plus one self-attested photocopy of each are required:
• Birth Certificate (Municipal Corporation / Hospital issued)
• Aadhar Card of the child
• Parent/Guardian Aadhar Cards (both, if available)
• 4 recent passport-sized photographs of the child
• Proof of Residence (utility bill, bank statement, or rental agreement — dated within 3 months)
• Pre-school/Kindergarten completion certificate (if applicable)
• Any medical records relevant to special needs or allergies

## For Class 2–12 Lateral Admissions

All documents from the new Class 1 list, plus:
• Transfer Certificate (TC) from the previous school, countersigned by the issuing school principal
• Original Report Card / Mark Sheet from the last attended class
• Character Certificate from the previous school
• Migration Certificate (for students transferring from a different state board or CBSE/ICSE to state syllabus)
• Caste Certificate (if applying under reserved category)

## For Scholarship Applicants

Students applying for scholarship concessions must additionally provide:
• Income Certificate issued by a revenue officer (not older than 6 months)
• Previous academic year's report card showing qualifying grades (Merit scholarships require 80%+)
• Relevant achievement certificates (Sports, Performing Arts, etc.) for category-specific scholarships
• EWS Certificate (for economically weaker section concessions)

## Document Submission Guidelines

• All documents must be submitted as originals for verification, along with one self-attested photocopy per document.
• Documents in languages other than English or the regional state language must be accompanied by an officially translated copy.
• Photocopies should be on A4 paper, clear and legible.
• The admissions team will return originals after verification — keep them safely.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What if my birth certificate is in another language?
You must provide an officially translated copy along with the original. Translations must be done by a certified translator or notarized authority.

### Can I submit documents digitally?
Physical copies are required for the initial submission and verification. After verification, digital copies can be uploaded to your student portal profile for record-keeping.
