# Admission Process & Guidelines

Official admission procedure and statutory guidelines for The VIDYA School, Sector 24, DLF Phase 3, Gurugram (CBSE Affiliation No. 531105). Admissions prioritize students from underprivileged communities for quality, subsidized English-medium education under Haryana Education Department regulations.

> [!NOTE]
> **Admission Status Notice (September 2026)**
> Current Academic Session 2026–27 classes are active (casual transfer admissions subject to seat availability). The formal registration schedule for Academic Session 2027–28 commences on 01 December 2026. For guidance, reach the Admissions Desk at +91-8130672281 or info.vidyaschool@vidya-india.org (Mon–Fri 8:00 AM – 4:30 PM).

| Admission Step | Procedure Description | Mandatory Documentation | Timeline & Session Cycle |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Step 1: Application Form Submission | Fill online application or collect paper form from school reception desk | Child Birth Certificate, 4 Passport Photos, Family Photo | 01 December – 15 February (Annual Intake) |
| Step 2: Document & Residence Verification | Verification of DLF Phase 3 / Gurugram residency and income certificate | Aadhaar Card of child & parents, Proof of Residence, Income Certificate / BPL Card | 16 February – 10 March |
| Step 3: Informal Child & Parent Interaction | Friendly informal interaction to assess grade readiness and student interests | Previous School Progress Card / Transfer Certificate (if applicable for Class II+) | 15 March – 25 March |
| Step 4: Orientation, Kit & Commencement | Welcome orientation, distribution of uniform kit, textbook sets, and school diary | Signed parent undertaking & medical fitness confirmation | First week of April (Session Commencement) |
