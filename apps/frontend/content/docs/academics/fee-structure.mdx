# Fee Structure & Scholarships (Academic Session 2026–27)

> [!NOTE]
> **Session Validity & Admission Status (Academic Year 2026–27)**
> The fee schedule detailed below is the official government-approved schedule applicable for Academic Session 2026–27 (continuing approved rates under Haryana School Education Department norms). VIDYA School is dedicated to providing high-quality English-medium CBSE education to children from underprivileged backgrounds; fees are heavily subsidized with full and partial philanthropic scholarships available. For active admission guidelines, please refer to the Admission Process & Schedule.

Official fee schedule approved for **The VIDYA School, Gurugram** (CBSE Affiliation No. 531105). In strict compliance with Haryana Government norms and CBSE bylaws, all fees are transparent, non-commercial, and capped to maintain accessibility for every deserving child.

> [!NOTE]
> **Zero Capitation Policy & 100% Refundable Caution Deposit**
> The VIDYA School strictly prohibits capitation fees, donations, or any hidden levies. A nominal one-time caution money of ₹750 is collected at the time of admission and is 100% refundable upon student clearance. Deserving students and those from economically weaker sections are supported through full scholarships and philanthropic partnerships.

### Important Regulations & Fee Policies

- **Refundable Caution Deposit:** The Caution Money of ₹750 collected at the time of admission is fully refundable when a pupil leaves the school, upon presentation of the original caution deposit receipt and complete clearance of all institutional dues.
- **School Development Charges:** Annual development charges of ₹600 are levied once per academic year towards maintaining digital smart classrooms, laboratories, libraries, and campus facilities.
- **Statutory Government Levies:** Haryana Govt Sports Fund, Red Cross Fund, and Children Welfare Fund are statutory collections remitted strictly to state authorities in compliance with Haryana Education rules.
- **Optional Transport:** School bus transport is strictly optional and billed at ₹1,000 per month only for students utilizing the designated bus routes.
- **Fee Remittance Schedule:** Monthly contributions must be remitted by the 10th of every calendar month through the online payment portal or at the school accounts desk.
- **100% Scholarships & Subsidies:** Deserving students and children facing extreme economic hardship are eligible for full tuition fee waivers, book sets, uniforms, and mid-day meal sponsorship through VIDYA's philanthropic donor network.
