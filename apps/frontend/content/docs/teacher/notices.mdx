How to create, format, and publish class announcements, exam schedules, and homework notices to student dashboards.

## Steps

### Step 01: Open the Notices Panel
Go to 'Notices' in your teacher sidebar. The panel shows all previously published notices and a 'New Announcement' button.

### Step 02: Create the Notice
Click 'New Announcement'. Fill in:
• Title (required): e.g., 'Maths Unit Test — Chapter 5 & 6'
• Body text (required): The full notice content. Use paragraph breaks for readability.
• Target classes: Select one or multiple classes that should receive this notice.
• Attachments (optional): Upload PDFs, images, or Word documents.

### Step 03: Set Priority
Toggle 'Mark as Urgent' for time-sensitive announcements (exam venue changes, emergency alerts). Urgent notices are displayed with a red warning badge at the top of the student's notice board and trigger an immediate push notification.

### Step 04: Publish
Click 'Publish'. The notice is instantly pushed to the notice boards of all students in the selected classes. A confirmation toast shows the notice ID and delivery count.

## Tips

- Write notice titles clearly — students see only the title in the notice list before clicking to expand.
- Schedule exam-related notices at least 7 days in advance to give students adequate preparation time.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I edit a notice after publishing?
Yes — click the notice in your notice list and select 'Edit'. Updates are reflected immediately on student boards. Students who already read the notice will see a 'Updated' badge.

### Can I target a specific section within a class?
Yes — the target selector lets you choose a full class (e.g., Class 10) or a specific section (e.g., Class 10-A only).
